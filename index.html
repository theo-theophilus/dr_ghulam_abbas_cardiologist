How do I request an appointment?

Use the appointment enquiry form on this page with your name, phone number and the reason for your visit. The clinic will follow up to confirm availability. Please do not include sensitive medical details in the form.

What should I bring to my first visit?

If available, bring a list of your current medications, recent test results or reports, any referral letter, and details of your medical history. This helps make the most of your consultation time.

Do I need a referral to see a cardiologist?

This depends on your clinic and insurance arrangements. Check when you request your appointment, and the team can tell you whether a referral is needed.

What happens during a cardiology consultation?

Your cardiologist will discuss your symptoms and medical history, review relevant tests, and talk through possible next steps. You will have the opportunity to ask questions and clarify anything you are unsure about.

What is coronary angiography?

Coronary angiography is a diagnostic procedure that uses imaging to assess blood flow through the heart's coronary arteries. Your clinician will explain why it may be appropriate, how to prepare and any risks before proceeding.

When should I seek emergency care?

If you have chest pain, severe shortness of breath, fainting or another symptom that feels urgent, contact your local emergency service immediately. Do not wait for a routine appointment or use this website's form for emergencies.
